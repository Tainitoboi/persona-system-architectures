Description

Tyler Gregory Okonma’s identity architecture presents a high-complexity, multi-layered system organized around a stable core and several derivative configurations. Rather than operating as a simple distinction between private individual and public persona, his identity unfolds as an evolving modular ecosystem structured through the interaction of alias, avatar, and alter ego. At the center of the system lies the original configuration, corresponding to the civil identity “Tyler Gregory Okonma.” This nucleus includes the nominal module (birth name), the representational module (the subject’s physical body), and the primary character disposition.

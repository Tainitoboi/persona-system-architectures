PERSONA SYSTEM ARCHITECTURES
ABOUT RESEARCH

Persona System Architectures is a platform dedicated to the exploration and critical analysis of contemporary identity structures. It examines how aliases, avatars, and alter egos operate within coherent yet distributed identity ecosystems, inviting users to observe, decode, and reflect on the design logics underlying Personas.

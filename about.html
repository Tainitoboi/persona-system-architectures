    Persona System Architectures is a digital archive designed to map and analyze identity structures through the methodological lens of the Persona System Design framework. The platform operates as both a research device and an exploratory interface, aimed at making visible the systemic and modular nature of personal identity configurations.

    The archive gathers case studies drawn from heterogeneous contexts, including public figures, fictional characters, artistic personas and mediated identities selected for their capacity to exemplify complex identity constructions. Rather than offering biographical descriptions, the website focuses on structural analysis, examining how identities are articulated, projected, and organized across different dimensions.

    Each entry is presented as an identity architecture. The goal of the platform is not to prescribe how identities should be constructed, but to provide a conceptual and analytical tool through which identity systems can be observed, compared, and critically interpreted. By translating theoretical research into a navigable digital environment, the archive bridges conceptual reflection and design practice.

    Conceived as an expandable system, the website supports comparative exploration and pattern recognition across cases. As identity configurations continuously evolve within contemporary media environments, the archive remains open to future implementation and theoretical refinement.

    Il sistema di classificazione si basa su parametri modulari definiti nel capitolo VI, assegnando livelli di completezza tramite codici colore (Verde, Blu, Rosa, Viola).
